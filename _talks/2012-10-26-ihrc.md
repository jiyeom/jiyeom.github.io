[SLIDE](https://jiyeom.github.io/files/ihrczashandout.pdf) In Korean, there are two <i>kes</i>-phrases that can denote human beings. One is a pseudocleft and the other is an IHRC. The bound noun <i>kes</i> corresponds to 'thing', and it is supposed to denote a non-human. Looking into these two constructions reveals that <i>kes</i> must denote a non-person as an object. In this talk, I deal with IHRCs. IHRCs can denote a stage or an individual depending on the predicate involved. This is against the idea of previous papers on IHRCs in Korean that an IHRC does not allow an individual-level predicate. When an IHRC looks as if it denoted a human being, it actually denotes a stage of a person. An IHRC that includes an individual-level predicate cannot denote a human being.
