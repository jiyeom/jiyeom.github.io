[PAPER](https://jiyeom.github.io/files/cil18final.pdf) Interpretation of numerals causes a lot of discussions because meanings of numerals are recalcitrant. I try to capture semantic and pragmatic aspects of numerals by assuming that the context is structured and semantic strength works in each sub-context but the sub-contexts are ordered based on informativeness which is different from semantic strength.
