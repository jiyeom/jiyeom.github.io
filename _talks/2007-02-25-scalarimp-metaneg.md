[SLIDE](https://jiyeom.github.io/files/metakyoto.pdf) This talk is against Chierchia's (2012) claim that scalar implicatures are locally calculated as grammatical phenomena. Generally, a scalar implicature is generally assumed to be calculated by the semantic negation of a stronger alternative proposition, but I claim that a scalar implicature is calculated by metalinguistic negation, which allows us to generate multiple scalar implicatures and factors like informativeness, plausibility, etc. select one in an actual context.
