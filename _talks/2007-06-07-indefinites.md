[PAPER](https://jiyeom.github.io/files/korspecfin2.pdf) In this paper I discuss two markers for specificity, <i>etten</i> and <i>han</i>, in
Korean. First, I discuss their structural properties and claim that <i>etten</i> is a determiner, and <i>han</i> is a numeral preceded by an implicit existential quantifier. Then I discuss four uses of <i>etten</i> and two uses of Mi>han</i>, and show when they are used as specificity markers. There are various properties of the two specificity markers, some of which they have in common. I take them as properties of specificity markers in general. The properties only one of them has are taken to come from the original meanings of the two morphemes. I claim that specific indefinites range over individual concepts, which lead to the properties they have in common.
