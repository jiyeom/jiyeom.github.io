In this paper, I claim that a presupposition generates a set of interpretations with various scopes and the strongest reading is selected among the ones allowed in the context. In some sense, presupposition projection is not a pragmatic phenomenon but a semantic one. This is included in one chapter of my Ph.D. thesis.
