[SLIDE](https://jiyeom.github.io/files/LIpres2.pdf) In wh-questions, a preposed wh-phrase should be reconstructed, but it cannot be because of the intervention effect. By interpreting a wh-phrase as ranging over non-contextual meanings, a preposed wh-phrase can get the effect of reconstruction semantically. Then a preposed wh-phrase will behave like a wh-phrase in situ. A wh-phrase in situ can be associated with an interrogative Mood over a syntactic island by being interpreted as generating a set of alternative non-contextual meanings. Since a non-contextual meaning is scopeless, the scope of a wh-phrase cannot be explained by a wh-movement or association with an interrogative Mood. The scope is determined depending on where the non-contextual meaning(s) are contextualized. I assume that a wh-phrase is a specific indefinite and the quantificational scope and the descriptive content are presupposed and the scope is determined depending on where the presuppositions are accommodated.
