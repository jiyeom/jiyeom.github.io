excerpt: 'In this paper, I will look at two conditional connectives in Korean and point out diﬀerences between <i>-(u)myen</i> and <i>-ta/la-myen</i> in their distributions and semantics. One of the diﬀerences is that <i>-ta/la-myen</i> always allows epistemic interpretation, whereas <i>-(u)myen</i> allows epistemic interpretation only when the event time of the antecedent clause is in the past or present. A second diﬀerence is that only <i>-(u)myen</i> is used in purely temporal and habitual conditionals. A third diﬀerence is that the modality marker <i>-keyss</i>, which can have volitional or predictive interpretation with <i>-(u)myen</i>, cannot have predictive interpretation with <i>-ta/la-myen</i>. I propose that <i>-ta/la-myen</i> has the operator of settledness, which is defined with respect to the speech time, and explain the diﬀerences listed based on the semantics of settledness.'
