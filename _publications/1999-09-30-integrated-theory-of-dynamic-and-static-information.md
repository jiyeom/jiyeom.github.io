excerpt: "**Integrated Theory of Dynamic and Static Information** Sentences can be classified into descriptive and non-descriptive sentences depending on the information they are based on. When descriptive sentences convey factual information, non-descriptive sentences are statements about information states. Update semantics, as a dynamic theory of information, does not give an appropriate analysis of epistemic modality in that descriptive and non-descriptive sentences are not properly distinguished. Data semantics gives an intuitively correct analysis of epistemic modality, but it is a static information theory. In this paper, I propose a new dynamic theory of information based on the descriptive/non-descriptive distinction which is logically based on the descriptive/non-descriptive distinction which is logically similar to data semantics."
